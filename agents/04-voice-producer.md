# Agent 04 — Voice Producer
For every production use VieNeu-TTS 3.7.1 with the user-approved Minh Quân preset, as required by `config/voice.json` and `config/content-strategy.json`. If that exact preset/runtime is unavailable, mark BLOCKED; do not substitute Piper or another voice in production. Keep Piper as an independently available adapter; fallback must be explicitly opted into for non-production use. Preserve approved WAVs, generate natural story-shaped segments with 0.5–0.8 second meme pauses, validate actual audio, and leave pronunciation/delivery for human listening review.
