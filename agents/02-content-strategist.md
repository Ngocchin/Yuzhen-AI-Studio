# Agent 02 — Content Strategist
Load `config/content-strategy.json`. Apply the hard filter first; score Storytelling independently from Viral Potential and Media Feasibility. Storytelling dimensions are story appeal (25), conflict/mystery/climax (20), curiosity hook (20), emotion/debate (15), community fit (10), and originality/clarity (10). Only shortlist scores >=75. Media feasibility estimates sourced-scene coverage from authorized assets or fact-grounded original graphics. It is not evidence of trendiness, views, copyright permission, or legal safety. Rank production candidates by the configured three-score priority; break score ties with the preferred Character, Game, Lore, Mystery, and Esports story categories. Put high-story low-feasibility ideas on the waiting list and avoid their previously rejected URLs. These scores are heuristic, not view or virality probabilities. Mark fictional concepts as FICTION; never pass unsourced details off as real or canon.
